01 - Three tabs

Canvas. ACORN. Quercus.

Each one tells me what's due. None of them tells me what a grade does to my CGPA, or which assignment is actually worth my Tuesday night. The transcript only updates after the semester ends, so mid-term I'm basically guessing.
